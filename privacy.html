Privacy Policy for BudgetZen

Last Updated: January 2024

📱 Important Notice: BudgetZen is a 100% local application. All your data stays on your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

1. Data Collection & Storage

No Data Collection: BudgetZen does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All your financial information remains exclusively on your device.

Local Storage Only: The following data is stored locally on your device:

  • Financial transactions (income and expenses)
  • Budget limits and categories
  • Project goals and savings targets
  • Application preferences (theme, currency, language)
  • Premium purchase status (managed by Google Play)

⚠️ Backup Responsibility: Since all data is stored locally, you are responsible for backing up your device. We recommend regular backups through your device's backup system.

2. Premium Features & In-App Purchases

Google Play Store: All premium purchases (subscriptions and one-time purchases) are processed through Google Play Store. We do not handle payment information.

Purchase Data: Google Play provides us with anonymous purchase verification data only (purchase status, product ID, expiration date). No personal payment information is shared with us.

3. Account Information

No Account Required: BudgetZen does not require user accounts. There is no registration, login, or password system.

No Email Collection: We do not collect email addresses, names, or any personal identifiers.

4. Permissions & Device Access

BudgetZen may request the following permissions:

  • Storage Access: To export data as CSV/Excel files
  • Notifications: To send budget alerts and reminders
  • Internet (optional): Only for Google Play purchase verification

All permissions are optional and clearly explained when requested.

5. Third-Party Services

Google Play Services: Used only for:

  • Processing in-app purchases
  • Purchase restoration when changing devices
  • Subscription management

We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking tools.

6. Data Security

Device Security: Your data is protected by your device's built-in security (encryption, lock screen).

No External Transmission: Financial data never leaves your device, not even in encrypted form.

7. Children's Privacy

BudgetZen is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

Contact Information

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at:

Email: support@budgetzen.app

Website: BudgetZen Legal Pages

This privacy policy complies with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations.
